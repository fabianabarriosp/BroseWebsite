What's in
the bottle?

Dry rosé made from Grenache, Tempranillo and Syrah. Expect bright strawberry, red cherry and citrus notes with a crisp, clean finish.

From grape
to glass

Red grapes are gently pressed and kept briefly with their skins to create BROSÉ's signature pale-pink colour. The juice is then cool-fermented to keep it fresh, fruity and crisp.

✦

Pairs well
with...

Perfect with grilled meats, pizza, seafood, spicy food and charcuterie. Serve chilled and let the food do the talking.
